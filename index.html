Our Story

The Home of True Colombo Flavors

Widely recognized by regulars as Akka Kade, Pradeep Food Center has earned its reputation through consistent taste, fresh spices, and fast service. Located at 12 Stace Rd, Colombo 14, we bring food lovers together for unforgettable street food and home-style dishes.

Whether you are craving late-night Kottu, a hearty plate of Rice & Curry, or savory Chinese-style fried rice, our menu is designed to satisfy every appetite.
